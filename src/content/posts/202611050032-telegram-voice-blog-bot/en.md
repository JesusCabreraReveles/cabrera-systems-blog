> With AI, I can send a voice note from Telegram and publish it to my blog and all my social networks in one go.

Social media can take up more time than you'd like. So I decided to use artificial intelligence to cut down that load.

## A blog for everything that crosses my mind

I built a small blog where I drop my ideas as they come. From there, I can export the content to other networks: LinkedIn, Instagram and X.

## A bot connected to Telegram

I created a bot and connected it to Telegram. I send it a voice note, it listens to what I want to publish and turns it into text. It's very practical because it's so simple.

## From audio to blog

The bot uploads the text directly to my personal blog. It also leaves it ready to copy and paste into other social networks.

If I want, I can add photos, videos or other elements to the post.

## The goal: publish to every network from Telegram

The interesting part is the goal: to push the content to all the networks at once from Telegram.
